Jason,

I reviewed your results, and I want you to hear the good news first: this is prevention work, not damage control. We have useful information, the important decisions are visible, and we are making those decisions from a position of time and choice.

Two findings still change how I would manage your plan. The ATM variant is not a cancer diagnosis, but it does mean we should be more intentional about surveillance. The elevated Lp(a) is not something you caused, but it does mean I want to lower the parts of cardiovascular risk we can actually change.

That is the balance I want you to take from this: there are real items to act on, but they are organized and manageable. For this week, please focus on three things: complete the baseline PSA, start rosuvastatin, and pick a time for the coronary calcium scan.

We will keep the longer-horizon items, including genetic counseling, pancreatic surveillance at age 50, and the 8-week lipid recheck, organized in the plan so they do not become today's burden.

You should feel good that we are finding the places where a deliberate step now can keep the future simpler. Message me if anything feels unclear or if you want to talk through the statin before starting.
