Marketing DS v1

Design system baseline

Do not drift from future capacity.

This is the working board for Aleron MD marketing. It defines what new explorations should reuse: visual temperature, typography, proof posture, modules, and the lines we do not cross.

Brand rules before components.

The goal is not to make another page. The goal is to give future pages a shared center of gravity.

Rule 01

Human outcome first.

Start with the life someone wants to keep available. Use data and product details only after the stakes are clear.

Rule 02

Physician led.

AI is leverage for medical judgment. It should never become the hero or the accountable actor.

Rule 03

Warm proof.

Evidence should be precise and understandable, with units and boundaries. No vague longevity fog.

Image treatment rules.

Photography should feel specific, human, and lived-in. Product shots should clarify the care loop, not become generic app marketing.

Use imagery that

  • Feels like an actual person, place, or care moment.
  • Supports future capacity, family, movement, recovery, or physician trust.
  • Has natural light, restrained contrast, and enough negative space for editorial layouts.
  • Avoids abstract AI brains, glowing networks, and generic wellness stock.

Drift checks.

This is the useful part: a visual test for whether new concepts still belong to Aleron MD.

Do not

Do
