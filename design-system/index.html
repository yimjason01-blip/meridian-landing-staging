Aleron MD

A warm, editorial system for premium preventive care.

Extracted from the Figma hero and website sections. The language is warm-editorial with clinical trust: a light, paper-toned canvas, ink and navy for weight, rust and gold as the only chromatic accents, and a Swiss-grid block motif over warm photography. Where Figma is still incomplete, primitives below fill the gaps in the same voice. This file is the anchor — build to it.

Geometric Blocks

The hero's signature: hard-edged color blocks composited over warm photography, Swiss-grid style. Terracotta and navy slabs, the occasional translucent gold band. Blocks stay square; only CTAs and chips are round.

Governance

House Rules

Carried from how Aleron has been built so far. Break these and the system drifts.

No invented gradients or glows
Gradients and color fades stay Figma-locked. Don't add fades, glows, or neon the source doesn't have.
No rounded-pill eyebrows
Eyebrow labels are plain uppercase text, not pills. Pills are for CTAs and status chips only.
No header CTA clusters
A single Log In top-right. Apply belongs in the hero. Never "Join". Avoid slider-tab-looking corner clusters.
Header ≥ body, always
Body text is never larger or bolder than the header above it. No inverted hierarchy.
Color carries meaning
Monochrome is the base. Rust/gold = brand accent; status colors = state only. Never decorative color.
Square blocks, round CTAs
The geometric block motif is hard-edged. Don't round the slabs; don't square the buttons.
Warmth never leaves
Even dark sections are warm (umber/ink), not cold gray. The cool ink is reserved for the hero rain only.
Real copy, no lorem
Past the sketch phase, use real Aleron voice. Patient stories stay in the patient story, no melodrama.
