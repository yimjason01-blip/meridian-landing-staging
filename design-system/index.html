Icon language

Two colors or it fails.

Icons bridge hard computational structure with warm analog care. They should feel like modular plan symbols, cut-paper apertures, dithered marks, or hatched clinical diagrams. If an icon needs more than two colors to work, it is not part of this system.

Approved

One-color or two-color pictograms, square containers, rust and ink, navy and cream, simple plan symbols.

Rejected

HUD anatomy, transparent bodies, glowing AI networks, dark analytic mysticism, wireframe people, multi-color medical tech icons.

Governance

What keeps this from drifting.

No uppercase branch leakageThe black square uppercase A is not a source for this system.
No rounded CTAsButtons are square-edge. The Figma hero does not license pill buttons.
No invented fadesUse the favicon fade, Figma hero fade, login fade, or curve palette.
No JoinUse Apply for Membership in CTA slots and Log In in the header.
No dashboard defaultProof modules stay editorial and legible, not SaaS analytics.
No generic clinical imageryPhotography must feel specific, warm, and human.
